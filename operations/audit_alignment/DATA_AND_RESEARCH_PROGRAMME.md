- **Fundamental conditional logit/multinomial logit:** build on #242's functional benchmark, with richer verified history. Race-level likelihood and shrinkage controls; never independent runner train/test splitting. Modern alternatives must add later evidence relative to this simple baseline.
- **Benter:** preserve existing fitted two-stage comparisons. Fit combination after independent-form training on unseen calibration races; no arbitrary fixed/dynamic weight. Assess complementary information even if standalone form has larger loss than market.
- **Hierarchical Bayes:** investigate partial pooling of trainer,jockey,horse,sire,lineage,trainer–jockey and track/distance effects. Start empirical-Bayes/regularised pooled controls, priors on scale, prior predictive/sensitivity checks, sparse/new entity performance and computational cost. Verify full methods before a claimed replication. Public metadata for Browne–Draper2006 is checked; no full-method implementation claimed here.
- **Plackett–Luce:** Plackett1975 probability model for rankings; compare its sequential likelihood with existing Harville/conditional-logit relationships rather than calling them unrelated inventions. No rerun of unchanged sparse data to claim new independent skill.
