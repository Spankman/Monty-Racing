### Freshness and card reconciliation

The [provider freshness contract](https://puntersedge.online/developers/freshness) defines poll `last_update` as the last bookmaker read, including a read confirming an unchanged price. Its live and card refresh tiers use different polling cadences. Price-change timestamps, push receipt timestamps and HTTP retrieval times cannot serve as bookmaker poll timestamps. The old adapter used the oldest admitted poll as the shared capture time. This could expire newer races along with the oldest one. The corrected code records when the board was acquired and checks the original oldest poll independently for each race. A regression verifies that a still-current race survives expiry of another race.
