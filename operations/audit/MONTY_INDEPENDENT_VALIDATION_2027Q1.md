# Monty AU independent validation protocol

Protocol version 1 · registered in GitHub draft before outcomes · 26 November 2026 Brisbane.

## Locked window and cohort

Prospective test window: 4 January–28 March 2027 inclusive by Brisbane date. Census denominator: every Queensland thoroughbred race listed in the official state calendar within the window, with scheduled start and revision timestamps. Register the race before the cutoff. Record every non-admission, including unavailable evidence and transport failures. Exclude trials, abandoned races, races with unresolved scratchings/reserves/identities, non-single winners for win scoring, and incomplete or disputed markets. Keep a ledger for late changes, settlement exclusions and counts by reason. All seven primary arms must use the identical admitted race and runner set. Do not silently drop empty days, replace blocked races, widen dates or stop early. No results exist for this cohort at publication.

Schedule split in Brisbane dates: fit through 29 November 2026, calibration 1–27 December, then freeze and publish a pre-test manifest before 4 January 2027. Strictly require source features and outcomes to have been available at their earlier respective decision and fit endpoints. If lawful verified training/calibration sets, receipts, candidate manifest or required official and named-bookmaker evidence cannot be ready on time, log BLOCKED for the window. A replacement cohort requires a fresh preregistration before any outcomes. No credentials, API requests, scheduled workflows or approval implied by this registration.
