## What was recovered

The original restored `joined_development_runners.csv.gz` has 62,255 rows across 6,422 AU markets, 1 January to 15 June 2025. Every row has the original scheduled-off best-back price, full-field normalized probability and original Betfair selection and market identifiers. It contains 52,674 distinct selection IDs and 14,541 normalized names mapping to multiple IDs across the file. Those collisions could include provider IDs that changed, reused names and cross-source identity variation. A single normalized name is insufficient evidence to join histories. These are the original joined historical inputs. The separately recovered official monthly exchange files extend the exact market fields to October 2026, without the full matched contextual inputs or validated contemporary provider-to-registered-to-Betfair crosswalk. V6 ingestion also used the wider V5 retained event history for feature construction, including prior events beyond the recovered joined file. Locate the hash-pinned V5 cleaned input and original Betfair data from the private project archives before claiming end-to-end reconstruction. The checkpoint is kept private.

The [55-feature register](Monty_V6_55_Feature_Register.csv) enumerates definitions, active columns, availability, historical windows, leakage controls and both individual ablation directions. In the archived live artifact every scored runner had 16 of 55 recorded as observed. V6 used 43 active inputs, of which 13 were observed in the mask. Of the 39 absent features 30 were active and nine were inactive in V6. Three of the 16 marked present were inactive. Actual going presence is separate from the six encoded indicators. A valid all-zero known-going vector plus an unknown indicator can mean the genuine condition was missing. Current intake can also expose claim and effective weight when explicit, but the frozen V6 coefficients do not use them.

2. **Extend the recovered prior market inputs.** May 2025–October 2026 is available through the official monthly exports. Cover the earliest retained history and November 2026 onward using original scheduled-off Betfair field snapshots or a matching quote stream. Confirm active population, scheduled-off cutoff, quote definition and selection mapping. Bits of current form, official SP, Betfair BSP and Krok bookmaker closing offers are different measurements. Preserve source market IDs and eligibility criteria, with a gap ledger through current dates. Earlier retained starts are necessary even for deterministic days-since-run and distance-change features.
3. **Prioritise contextual recovery.** Track residual first, then distance and going residuals, trailing-year horse form, 90-day jockey residual and benchmark-class change. Track/distance/going history depends on prior market probabilities and verified historical conditions. Do not take end-of-meeting going from a result page as the earlier race's condition. Capture real earlier runner/connection declarations and completed results separately. Review current benchmark parsing as its own easy acquisition candidate.
4. **Defer gear, age and weight enrichment as an accuracy project.** These have no coefficient in frozen V6. Some are useful as evidence and for a challenger study. Do not buy them to improve the unchanged scorer.

## Betting approval and dynamic weighting

Use two axes. Prediction status reports **PROVISIONAL** for genuine V6 calculations without complete validation and **RESEARCH ONLY** for V7. Decision status uses **DATA BLOCKED** for essential verification failures, expiry or missing calibrated evidence, **EXECUTION FAILED** for technical failures, **NO BET** only once a valid evaluator has enough evidence to conclude no usable value, and **QUALIFIED** only when that evaluator passes a valid candidate. A fresh assessment with an inconclusive uncertainty interval can remain provisional. Do not confuse a 100% feature count with proof of calibration and do not make optional inactive features a universal blocker.

For WIN candidates use calibrated point and lower/upper estimates, fair odds 1/p and net EV at the available named fixed price. Track prospective calibration by price, going/surface, field size and missingness profile with shrinkage for small strata. Compare at the quoted odds, then rerun or expire if the price moves or a runner withdraws. Test an economic decision boundary derived from break-even and measured price slippage/uncertainty. Keep an uncertainty hold when a positive edge is neither supported nor ruled out. Exotic decisions require separate joint outcome evidence and their own accounting. The $4 display progression remains based on verified WIN prices, never exotic estimates. The current evaluator can explain evidence and arithmetic only. No validated prospective thresholds, approval authorisation or genuine live NO BET validation were obtained.

Production executes at 100/0. The separate existing candidate `d36e015` admits only verified pre-race source groups, normalizes consensus by group, fits a market shrinkage term on prior settlements and tests 0/5/10/15/20% with a bounded adaptive arm. Its six contract tests passed locally. The audited research archive reports zero matched eligible settled expert races. Thus ROI, hit rate, calibrated uplift, drawdown and value comparison for 90/10, 80/20 and dynamic are unestimated. 100/0 is the only supported operational setting.

Extend the candidate after acquisition: derive per-expert scores from residual improvement against market, use partial pooling by jurisdiction/track/class/going and penalize favourite copying and common editorial origin. Compare market favorite overlap, discrimination against the normalized market, and subgroup calibration. Learn recency decay and confidence gates only inside earlier training folds. Freeze an outer chronological forecast before results, compare every arm on identical races and common quotes, and report both eligible matched and all-race fallback populations. Use paired day/meeting uncertainty, net ROI/drawdown, strike rate, Brier and log loss, with a controlled allowance for testing multiple weights. The current research branch uses more limited aggregate weighting. Specialisation and recent market-movement effects have no verified empirical performance yet.
