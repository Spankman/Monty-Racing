## Extended academic programme

- **Hierarchical shrinkage:** start by comparing a regularised one-stage conditional-logit baseline with a partially pooled role model. Predeclare shrinkage for trainer and jockey before horse, sire, trainer–jockey and course/distance interactions. Require repeated identities, identifiability and enough race contexts. Report posterior/predictive calibration, outcomes for low-count roles, out-of-time log loss, prior sensitivity and compute cost. Conditional logit with frailty is a useful related reference, with any adaptation stated.
- **Finishing order:** compare Harville constructed from locked win probabilities, classic stagewise Plackett–Luce fit from official complete or explicitly partial rankings, and a rank-stage-varying challenger only with sufficient sample. A fixed-worth Plackett–Luce sequential ranking has the same probability form as Harville given the same win-worth vector. Distinguish different estimation data or stage-dependent behaviour from a mere relabel. Treat withdrawals, partial results and dead heats explicitly. Validate top-k probability and order likelihood, calibrated place and exotics with correct terms; no operational use without eligible joint odds/results and owner approval.
- **Favourite–longshot:** report residual reliability/calibration by fixed odds band, race type, field size, track/meeting class, going, market maturity and time before jump. Note quote-time policy and overround/completeness. Use pooled coarse bins before sparse subdivisions. Compare raw and temperature-calibrated market with a pre-fitted smooth correction using earlier data only. Evaluate stability on later blocks and avoid interpreting output of market normalization as independent gain.
