## Collection order and rules
1. Reuse this register before searching for new sites. Prioritize permitted public pages with named authors, dated selections and meeting/race/runner details.
2. Check all AU/NZ TAB meetings, including country and NZ; log attempted sources and coverage gaps.
3. Store source URL, original author, source identity, actual UTC capture time, published timestamp when available, race off time from verified race feed, and horse identity. Never backdate a capture or infer a tip from a preview or quaddie anchor.
4. Deduplicate syndicated authors and cross-posted selections. Site count is not independent analyst count.
5. Reject post-off/unverifiable tips from blending. Preserve them as excluded research evidence only.
6. No expert data? Monty continues at 100% model weighting. The existing V7 shadow coverage and safety gates still apply.
7. Respect robots, access terms, paywalls and licensing. No credential bypass.

This is a persistent discovery register, **not an automated collector**.
