## Sources and actionable hypotheses
- Benter (1994), https://gwern.net/doc/statistics/decision/1994-benter.pdf — PDF excerpts reviewed. Fit race-normalised probabilities proportional to exp(a log(p_form) + b log(p_market)); estimate combination on out-of-fold form predictions. Compare incremental log likelihood over market alone. His reported example shows expert consensus added much less than independent form, despite similar standalone performance. Test expert signals for incremental value, not popularity.
- Bolton & Chapman (1986), https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.32.8.1040 — abstract reviewed; full PDF located at https://www.fsb.miamioh.edu/lij14/411_read_horse.pdf. Race-grouped multinomial logit and rank information; holdout evaluation.
- West & Kazakov (2024), https://doi.org/10.5750/jpm.v18i1.2093 — abstract reviewed in current session. Australian partial least squares approach to correlated variables and class imbalance; reproduce methodology after full-text review. Reported returns are not independently validated.
- Sung & Johnson, https://doi.org/10.5750/jpm.v1i1.419 — abstract reviewed. Compare one-stage and two-stage conditional logit.
- Lessmann, Sung & Johnson, https://doi.org/10.5750/jpm.v1i3.427 — abstract/PDF excerpts reviewed. Evaluate top-rank metrics rather than treating every finishing position equally.
- Lessmann, Sung & Johnson (2010), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2009.12.013 — abstract reviewed. Compare race-aware random forests and conditional logit; model relative opponent strength.
- Outstanding: locate and verify Silverman/Suchard regularised conditional logit and Sugiura 2026 feature and uncertainty studies; complete full-text reads of all above, noting access limitations. No paid access without approval.

## Second literature review — 25 November 2026
Sources reviewed to the extent accessible; not an exhaustive reading or independent replication.
- Smith & Vaughan Williams (2010), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2009.12.014 — abstract and article excerpts: favourite-longshot bias varied over ten UK seasons. Test calibration by Australian decision-time odds bands, class, provider and period; report counts and uncertainty rather than importing UK coefficients.
- Clarke, Kovalchik & Ingram (2017), https://doi.org/10.11648/j.ajss.20170506.12 — PDF abstract/introduction reviewed: compare proportional, power and Shin de-vig on same complete bookmaker fields. Power solves sum((1/odds)^k)=1. Fit/compare on chronological data; separate margin-adjusted market probability from actual offered-price break-even. Low-cost experiment using existing odds.
- Spence et al. (2012), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3391435/ — methods/results/discussion excerpts reviewed: pacing and drafting associations across 44,803 starts, 3,357 UK races. Test sectionals, race-pace interactions and wide/no-cover indicators only where observed; do not infer drafting from finish rank. Associations are not causal proof or betting edge.
- Benter (1994), https://ryansbrill.com/pdf/statistics_in_sports_papers/Horse_Race_Betting_Benter1994.pdf — factor-development section reviewed: residual performance by distance and uncertainty adjustment. Test cross-fitted residual suitability for distance, going, course, fitness and connections with shrinkage; all historic predictions and aggregates strictly as-of. Avoid using outcome of target race.
- Lessmann et al. (2010), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2009.12.013 — abstract and accessible article sections: race-aware random forest evaluated on 1,000 HK races. Audit field-relative variables and compare simple grouped logit before complex alternatives.
- Silverman & Suchard (2013), https://doi.org/10.5750/jpm.v7i1.595 — journal abstract paywalled; free dissertation chapter located and excerpt reviewed: https://escholarship.org/content/qt9tm6w0rp/qt9tm6w0rp_noSplash_490c4af635510cf20ca3a62a7156f5d4.pdf?t=n0kjxz . Test standard regularisation before profit-target tuning; avoid importing random split methodology, require temporal nested tests and untouched prospective forecasts.
- Sugiura (2026), https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2026.1896192 — abstract/introduction reviewed: independent test 4,556 races; matched simpler model win AUC 0.7543 vs augmented 0.7293 (0.7224 is lower CI, not point estimate). Place AUC 0.7513 vs 0.7164. No proof that all domain knowledge is harmful; perform matched feature-block ablations and race-cluster uncertainty estimation. No demonstrated betting profitability from AUC.
Prioritise existing-code audit and low-cost market-calibration experiments before costly feature acquisition. Consolidate into existing benchmark programme; no new standalone pipelines unless justified. No change to V6/V7, no spend, no merge.
